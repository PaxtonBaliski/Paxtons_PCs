About

Hello there, I'm Paxton Baliski

I'm passionate about building custom PCs, Keyboards & more that perfectly match your needs and budget. Whether you're a gamer seeking maximum performance, a content creator needing reliable power, or someone looking for a solid dailydriver, I'll guide you through every step of the process.

With years of hands-on experience in PC building and troubleshooting, I take pride in delivering quality systems backed by expert knowledge and personalized service. Every build is carefully assembled and thoroughly tested to ensure it runs flawlessly.

  • ✓ Expert Hardware Selection: I'll help you choose the perfect components for your specific needs and budget, avoiding common pitfalls and incompatibility issues.
  • ✓ Quality Assembly & Testing: Every system is built with care, cable management included, and thoroughly tested before delivery.
  • ✓ Ongoing Support: Get help even after your build is complete. I'm here for upgrades, troubleshooting, and technical questions.
